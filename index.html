The problem

A neural network inside a feedback loop can turn small errors into unsafe behavior. Guarantees, not just good average performance, are what safety-critical autonomy needs.

The approach

Bound the network with sector bounds, recast the closed loop as a positive Lur’e system, then certify safety with simple linear-algebra checks.

The payoff

Certificates that scale — up to ~104× faster than SDP methods, and robust to real-world delays and uncertainty.

About

Trustworthy, scalable AI for systems that move.

I am a Ph.D. researcher at Northeastern University developing foundational methods for trustworthy and scalable artificial intelligence in dynamical systems. My work lives at the intersection of data-driven learning, optimization, and control theory, with a focus on formal verification, robustness, and safety guarantees for neural-network-based decision-making.

I build the theory, the algorithms, and the software to certify learning-enabled systems operating in complex, safety-critical environments — with applications spanning autonomous systems and robotics. The goal is simple to state and hard to deliver: mathematical proof that an AI controller keeps its system safe.

Research

Making the loop certifiable.

When a neural network sits inside a feedback loop, verifying safety means reasoning about the whole closed system at once. My pipeline turns that hard problem into tractable checks — and keeps the guarantee under delays and uncertainty.

Sector bounds

Neural network bounding

Γ₁ x ≤ NN(x) ≤ Γ₂ x

I derive linear sector bounds that tightly enclose a network’s nonlinear behavior — global bounds for whole-state-space analysis and local, input-aware bounds that beat CROWN-style methods while staying compatible with control tools.

IEEE L-CSS 2024
Positive Lur’e

Stability & safety certification

By embedding the bounded network in a positive Lur’e system, certifying stability reduces to checking whether simple matrices are Metzler and Hurwitz — no large SDPs. This runs up to ~104× faster and also estimates the region of attraction.

IEEE CDC 2025
Delays · Uncertainty

Risk-aware verification

Real systems face communication and computation delays and modeling error. I certify safety across all delay values with positivity-based certificates, and provide a high-fidelity IQC benchmark for worst-case robustness under structured uncertainty.

ACC 2026 · arXiv
Multi-agent

Crowd dynamics & evacuation

A parallel line of work in public safety: the Predator–Swarm–Guide model for steering crowds to safety under threat. I tune guidance strategies through nonlinear optimization to reduce casualties, backed by continuum-limit analysis.
